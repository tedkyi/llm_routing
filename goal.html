Framing the problem

Goal

  • The goal of LLM routing has evolved and become more sophisticated
  • Earliest idea was simply: here’s my prompt, what is the cheapest LLM which can handle it?
  • Complexity added when you consider the human cost of failures, incorrect answers, suboptimal answers, and hallucinations
  • Complexity added when tasks are agentic instead of one-shot
  • Complexity added in multi-turn or with other contextual info beyond the current prompt

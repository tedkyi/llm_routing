Measuring routers

Oracle Performance

  • Several benchmarks score every prompt on every model in the pool, then report the oracle ceiling — the accuracy you would reach if you always picked the right model: RouterBench, LLMRouterBench (Li et al., 2026), RouterArena (ICLR 2026)
  • The Routing Plateau: Understanding and Breaking the Accuracy Limits of LLM Routers (Lu et al., 2026) — 21 routing methods across five benchmarks — arxiv.org/abs/2606.07587
    • On RouterBench the top 15 routers differ by 0.23 percentage points — diverse designs, one shared ceiling
    • Plain kNN ranks in the top 2 on all five benchmarks
    • Hard queries are 11–35% of each benchmark but account for 70–91% of the oracle gap
    • Best remedy so far: 10× more training data, a stronger encoder, and end-to-end fine-tuning together add 2.13 pp and close 14.6% of the gap
  • LLMRouterBench (400K+ instances, 21 datasets, 33 models): the best routers land a few points above the best single model, while the oracle sits roughly 20 points above the best router
  • Caveat worth stating out loud: about 36% of the reported router-to-oracle gap is single-draw label noise under stochastic decoding, which no single-commit router can capture (arXiv 2607.03436)
